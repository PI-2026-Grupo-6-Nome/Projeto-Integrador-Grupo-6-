About Us

Eduarda

Birth

Eduarda was born on the morning of June 14, 2010, a Monday, in Umuarama, Paraná, at Nossa Senhora Aparecida Hospital. I remember that the doctors were making guesses about her birth weight, and the one who guessed over 3 kg got it right! It was a very emotional day, filled with joy for the whole family.

Educational Journey

My educational journey began in Céu Azul, at Escola São Cristóvão, where I studied from the 1st to the 5th grade. Later, I moved to Nova Santa Rosa and continued my studies at Colégio Estadual Marechal Gaspar Dutra, where I completed elementary school. Currently, I am in the 1st year of high school at IFPR in Toledo, starting a new stage of my educational journey. Changing schools brought new challenges and experiences, contributing to my personal growth and to the path I have followed so far.

Historical Fact

Where We Live

Municipality – Nova Santa Rosa

Nova Santa Rosa is a municipality located in western Paraná, with a history shaped by the settlement of families who came mainly from Rio Grande do Sul, many of them of German descent. Colonization began in 1953, and the municipality achieved political and administrative independence in 1976. One of the town's cultural characteristics is its German influence, which can be seen in customs, traditions, and even in the language, which is still preserved by some families. Nova Santa Rosa is also home to the Dom Severino Kögl Municipal Museum, created to preserve objects, photographs, and documents related to the history of its first residents.

Neighborhood

I live in the Cidade Nova neighborhood, a quiet area of Nova Santa Rosa, made up mainly of houses and residential streets. It is a simple neighborhood where many people know each other and interact in their daily lives.
