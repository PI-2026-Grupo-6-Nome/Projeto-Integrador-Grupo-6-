Our Chronicle

Unconscious Consumption

Liana came from a family that struggled financially. From a young age, she knew what it was like not to be able to have everything she wanted. Because of this, she dedicated herself to her studies and did everything she could to graduate. Through hard work and dedication, Liana managed to graduate from a renowned institution and got a job at a large company. She quickly advanced in her professional career and began earning a good salary. Because of this, she felt that she finally had the right to buy everything she wanted, since she had not had that opportunity during her childhood.

A few months later, her house was turning into a huge storage space. Everything different that Liana saw in stores, she bought, because she felt she needed to take advantage of the opportunity to own things she had never been able to afford before. One morning, her mother came to visit her. When she noticed her daughter's situation and the amount of things piling up, she decided to talk to her. She told Liana that perhaps it was time to rethink her habits, since buying things impulsively was not the best way to enjoy her achievements. That was when Liana truly realized that she needed help. She sought psychological support and began reflecting on her choices and the importance of being aware of what she consumed. Gradually, she understood that shopping did not fill the emptiness she felt; it only provided temporary satisfaction.

With this change, Liana began to rethink her habits, sold the things she no longer used, and started buying only what she truly needed or what was meaningful to her. She also realized that her money could be used in more important ways, such as making plans for the future and helping people who faced difficulties similar to those she had experienced. Liana understood that being able to afford everything did not mean she needed to have everything. After all, conscious consumption does not mean giving up shopping, but learning to recognize what truly matters.
