About Us

Camile

Birth

Camile Vitória Blodorn was born at 5:50 p.m. on January 31, 2011, at Centro Hospitalar do Oeste Ltda, along with her twin brother, Bruno Henrique Blodorn. She was born measuring 42 cm and weighing 1.675 kg. It was a moment of great joy, but also concern, when everyone saw how tiny the two babies were. They were so small that both could fit in the same crib. Their older sister, Isabel, was very excited to meet her two little siblings. She had always been certain there would be two babies, a boy and a girl. They brought great joy to the entire family and to the town, being one of the first pairs of twins in the municipality.

Educational Journey

I started school at the age of four at Escola Municipal Professor Leopoldo Kuroli, where I studied until the 5th grade of elementary school, finishing in 2022. In 2023, I joined Colégio Estadual PIO XII, where I studied until the 9th grade. Now, I have started high school at IFPR – Federal Institute of Paraná, in Toledo, an extension of the Assis Chateaubriand Campus.

Historical Fact

My mother says that when I was born, in June, an intense cold wave affected our region. Temperatures dropped to nearly -3°C, with heavy frost, resulting in one of the coldest nights in the state in recent years.

Current Moment

I believe I have expanded my knowledge and discovered a new area of interest. Being part of IFPR has been a great opportunity to explore new paths and meet new people.

Future Perspectives

I hope to finish high school and then pursue a degree in Performing Arts, where I plan to specialize in this field.

Where We Live

Municipality – Maripá

Maripá originated on May 13, 1946, with the founding of Companhia Madeireira Rio Paraná S/A (MARIPÁ). It became an independent municipality from Palotina through State Law No. 9,226, on April 17, 1990. Maripá is known for its festivals, such as the "Orchid Festival" and the "Tractor Drag Racing Event", which attract visitors from all over Brazil and other countries. The town is also recognized for its agriculture and fertile soil.

Rural Community

This area is connected to the colonization and agricultural development process that began in the mid-20th century. This period was marked by the division of rural land into small properties and by the strong presence of family farming and dairy cattle raising.
