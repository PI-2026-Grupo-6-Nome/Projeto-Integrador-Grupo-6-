Welcome to CoIFinhos!

CoIFinhos is a project developed by students from the Federal Institute of Paraná (IFPR), aiming to highlight the importance of economics and conscious consumption in our community.

Here, we want to share information, ideas, and reflections on how our consumption choices influence the place where we live. After all, small actions can contribute to a more conscious community and a better future.

Get to Know Our Project

Economy and Conscious Consumption

What is economics?

Economics is part of our daily lives and involves how we use resources, produce, buy, and consume goods and services. It is also connected to the development of cities and people's quality of life.

What is conscious consumption?

Conscious consumption means thinking before buying, considering our needs, and understanding the impact of our choices. Actions such as avoiding waste, reusing materials, and supporting local products are ways to encourage more responsible consumption.

The local economy in our region

In municipalities such as Nova Santa Rosa and Maripá, agriculture plays an important role in the local economy. Agricultural production, together with local shops and small businesses, drives the region's economy and supports many families.

Supporting local farmers and shop owners, avoiding food waste, and making responsible choices are ways to strengthen our community's economy.

How can we make a difference?

We can start with simple actions: planning our purchases, making better use of food, reusing materials whenever possible, saving resources, and choosing local businesses.

Small choices make a difference. Conscious consumption is also a way of taking care of our community and our future!
