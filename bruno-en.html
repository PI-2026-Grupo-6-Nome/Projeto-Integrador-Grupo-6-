Where We Live

Municipality – Maripá

Maripá originated on May 13, 1946, with the founding of Companhia Madeireira Rio Paraná S/A (MARIPÁ). It became an independent municipality from Palotina through State Law No. 9,226, on April 17, 1990. Maripá is known for its festivals, such as the "Orchid Festival" and the "Tractor Drag Racing Event", which attract visitors from all over Brazil and other countries. The town is also recognized for its agriculture and fertile soil.

Rural Community

This area is connected to the colonization and agricultural development process that began in the mid-20th century. This period was marked by the division of rural land into small properties and by the strong presence of family farming and dairy cattle raising.
